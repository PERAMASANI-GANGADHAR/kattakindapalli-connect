# 🌾 KKP - Mana Ooru

## 📍 Village

Katta Kinda Palli  
Anantapur District, Andhra Pradesh, India

---

# 🌍 Vision

To transform Katta Kinda Palli into a smart, transparent, and digitally connected village using technology.

---

# 🎯 Mission

Build one digital platform where villagers, farmers, students, and the Panchayat can work together to improve village life.

---

# 🚀 Version 1 Features

## 🏡 Village

- Report Problems
- Track Complaint Status
- Village Announcements
- Emergency Contacts

## 👨‍🌾 Farmers

- Weather
- Government Schemes
- Crop Information

## 🎓 Students

- Digital Library
- Scholarships
- Job Notifications

## 👨‍💼 Admin

- Dashboard
- Complaint Management
- Progress Tracking

---

# ❤️ Long Term Goal

Expand from Katta Kinda Palli to every village in Andhra Pradesh and India.
